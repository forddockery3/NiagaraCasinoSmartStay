About the app

Niagara Casino Smart Stay is a hotel guest service iPhone application.

Purpose

Niagara Casino Smart Stay brings room requests, hotel event reservations, in-room dining, parking space reservations, hotel blog content, and a temporary guest code into one mobile interface. The application and its supporting website are developed by PATTERN BARBER SHOP LIMITED, with Niagara Entertainment Inc. identified in connection with the application.

Guests can submit and track service requests, reserve event places, order food to the room, manage parking reservations, and read hotel information during a stay — without switching between different tools.

Guest Requests

Seven categories from room comfort to special celebrations. Track Submitted → Accepted → In Progress → On the Way → Completed.

Events & dining

Reserve hotel events and order from Breakfast, Grill, or Light Bites with cart, optional note, and delivery estimate.

Parking & blog

Three parking floors with map and My Reservations. Hotel Blog articles with Favorites and sharing.

Guest code

A temporary guest code with no personal data — open full-screen for reception.

Platform

Designed for iPhone guests during a stay.
