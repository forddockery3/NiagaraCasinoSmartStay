What the app does

Everything you need during a hotel stay — submit and track requests, reserve event places, order food to your room, manage parking, and read hotel articles.

Guest Requests

Send service requests from the app: Room Comfort & Amenities, Housekeeping, Food & Beverage, Laundry, Maintenance, Transportation & Concierge, and Special Requests. Add a description of what you need, then track statuses from Submitted to Completed.

Event Booking

Browse hotel events with photo, description, date, start time, and available places — rooftop dinners, live music, tastings, brunch, outdoor movies, afternoon tea, poolside, and garden dinners. Reserve places and add an optional note.

In-Room Dining

Browse Breakfast & Morning Meals, Main Courses & Grill, and Light Bites & Snacks. Each dish shows photo, description, price, prep time, and ingredients. Build a cart and confirm your order.

Parking

Interactive map for Floor 1, 2, and 3. Check available and reserved spaces, pick a spot, set start and end times, and manage bookings in My Reservations — active, upcoming, and completed.

Hotel Blog

Articles on facilities, guest services, dining, events, parking, and stay tips. Each piece has a cover, title, preview, and full text. Save to Favorites or share.

Guest Code

A temporary guest code appears throughout the app. The short number changes periodically and contains no personal data. Open it full-screen when you need to show it at reception.
